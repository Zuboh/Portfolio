# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, technical leads, and fellow engineers evaluating Lorenzo Zubani as a frontend + AI engineering hire or collaborator. They open the site with a specific question: is this person worth my time? They are experienced enough to recognize craft and to be bored by templates. Secondary audience: open-source peers and the engineering community finding Lorenzo through GitHub or social.

## Product Purpose

zubo.dev is Lorenzo's primary professional artifact: a portfolio for a Frontend Engineer specializing in React and TypeScript, with strong attention to architecture, UX and implementation quality. It shows the work as case studies, the stack as evidence, and live commits as proof of ongoing activity. A recruiter should understand who he is and what he has built within a minute, and an engineer should be able to go deeper.

## Brand Personality

Precise, handcrafted, direct.

Voice: low-key, no hype, technically confident. Writes like an engineer who thinks clearly and doesn't oversell. Humor through detail, not jokes. The portfolio says "here's the proof" and steps aside.

## References

Vercel and its Geist design system: dark, typographic, hairline-divided, nearly no color. Product-engineering case studies that show architecture and decisions, not screenshots alone. Clearly authored by one person, not assembled from a template.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS template** — Framer/Webflow starters, hero sections with big metrics, blue gradient CTAs, "We help you build X faster" energy. This is a person, not a product.
- **Corporate consultant** — navy + gold, formal whitespace, stock imagery, professional-services formality. No warmth, no identity.
- **Creative developer overload** — 3D WebGL canvases, scroll-hijacking, dramatic particle effects that announce themselves. Creativity through restraint, not spectacle.

## Design Principles

1. **Here's the proof, nothing else.** The work is the argument. No marketing copy, no padded intros, no heroic self-description. Evidence precedes claim.
2. **Authored, not templated.** Precise typography, real content and live data signal that a person made this, not a template kit. Nothing is claimed that the repo or the résumé cannot back up.
3. **Quiet confidence.** Presence through restraint. No design element should shout. If something draws attention, it earns it through quality, not size or color.
4. **Reward the curious.** Visitors who look closely find more. The surface is clean; the depth is there for those who want it.
5. **Direct and complete.** If it can be cut, cut it. Every section answers a real question the visitor has.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. `prefers-reduced-motion` already respected at the CSS layer — maintain this across all new animation work. No accessibility-critical information conveyed by color alone. Keyboard navigability for all interactive elements.
